## This skill

The skill's own code and content (the engine, layouts, scripts, built-in theme
tokens, starter deck and documentation) is released under the MIT License. The
full text is in `LICENSE.txt`.

## Decks you build

A deck belongs to whoever made it. The build copies the engine and layouts into
each deck, and adds a one-line credit comment for them at the top of the file.
The authors of DynamicDecks treat that comment as sufficient notice for the copy of
the engine inside a deck; leave it in place when you share or sell one.

of this skill. The import
scripts record whatever license note the user gives and remind them when
